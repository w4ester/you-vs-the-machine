What this does—and does not—tell us

We use the rough English-language approximation 1 token ≈ 0.75 words and 52 weeks per year. Training tokens can include repetitions, code, multiple languages, and material unlike readable prose; the comparison does not measure unique texts or information retained. The exercise is a scale analogy only. Exposure ≠ comprehension, memory, or intelligence. Reading speed controls the estimated hours per reading day; daily pages control the total years. DeepSeek-V3 reports 14.8T training tokens; technical source. Qwen3 reports about 36T; announcement. Llama 3.1 reports over 15T; Meta source. Private-model counts are not supplied because exact comparable figures are generally undisclosed.
